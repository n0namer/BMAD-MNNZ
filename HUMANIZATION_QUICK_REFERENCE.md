# Humanization Copywriting Quick Reference

## ONE-PAGE CHECKLIST FOR HUMAN-SOUNDING COPY

### BEFORE YOU WRITE
- [ ] Research your audience deeply (vocabulary, fears, values)
- [ ] Interview 3-5 actual customers about their experience
- [ ] Create fact sheet about product/service
- [ ] Identify which emotions resonate most
- [ ] Understand the real problem you're solving

### WHILE WRITING (Draft Phase)
- [ ] Write conversationally first, not formally
- [ ] Use "you," "I," "we" throughout
- [ ] Include at least one personal story or example
- [ ] Use specific details and sensory language
- [ ] Vary sentence length (5 words to 30+ words)
- [ ] Show vulnerability or admitted limitation
- [ ] Read aloud to catch robotic phrasing

### WHILE EDITING (Refinement Phase)
- [ ] Remove 50% of transition words (moreover, furthermore, additionally)
- [ ] Replace formal words with simple ones (use, help, show instead of utilize, facilitate, demonstrate)
- [ ] Break up uniform paragraph lengths
- [ ] Verify every single claim and citation
- [ ] Add contractions (we're, don't, can't)
- [ ] Reduce hedge words (might, could, tends to, typically)
- [ ] Embrace strategic imperfection (fragments, tangents if authentic)

### FINAL CHECK
- [ ] Read aloud once more
- [ ] Does it sound like one human talking to another?
- [ ] Can I visualize the examples?
- [ ] Does it respect my intelligence?
- [ ] Do I feel understood?
- [ ] Is there personality and perspective?
- [ ] Are there specific details, not generalities?

## THE 6 ANTI-PATTERNS TO ELIMINATE

### 1. OVERUSED AI PHRASES
**Remove these (294,000x to 3,000x more common in AI):**
- delve, testament, realm, tapestry, serves as a
- in today's fast-paced world
- in the ever-evolving
- as a powerful reminder
- faced numerous challenges
- provides new insights into
- it is important to note that

**Replace with:** Direct language, conversational alternatives

### 2. EXCESSIVE HEDGING
**Remove these:**
- generally speaking
- tends to
- it could be argued
- to some extent
- from a broader perspective
- it's important to note

**Replace with:** Direct statements when you have evidence

### 3. REPETITIVE TRANSITIONS
**Remove 50% of:**
- Moreover, furthermore, additionally, consequently
- Opening consecutive paragraphs with same connector
- Stock phrases connecting ideas

**Replace with:** Let ideas flow naturally, use casual pivots (Still, On top of that, Even so)

### 4. UNIFORM SENTENCE LENGTH
**If all sentences are 12-18 words:**
- Create 5-word punch lines
- Create 30+ word complex thoughts
- Vary rhythm to match natural speech

### 5. FORMAL/LATINATE WORDS
**Change:**
- utilize → use
- facilitate → help
- demonstrate → show
- serves as → is
- features/offers → has

### 6. MISSING PERSONALITY
**Add:**
- Your actual perspective
- Personal story or experience
- Humor if authentic
- Admitted limitations
- Why you care about this

## QUICK FORMULA TEMPLATES

### SIMPLE: AIDA for Emails
1. **Attention:** Subject line that speaks to their specific need
2. **Interest:** Opening sentence with concrete benefit
3. **Desire:** Story or example of transformation
4. **Action:** One clear next step

### STRONG: PAS for Landing Pages
1. **Problem:** Name their specific pain in their language
2. **Agitation:** Show the cost of not solving it
3. **Solution:** Your answer with proof it works

### VERSATILE: Before-After-Bridge
1. **Before:** Their current situation (show empathy)
2. **After:** Transformed situation (be specific)
3. **Bridge:** How they get from before to after

### ENGAGING: Story Arc
1. **Hook:** Surprising or specific opening
2. **Challenge:** The struggle (make it relatable)
3. **Resolution:** How problem was solved
4. **Takeaway:** What reader learns/action they take

## THE KNOW-LIKE-TRUST FRAMEWORK

**Stage 1: KNOW** - Who are you?
- Share values and perspective
- Show expertise through teaching
- Be clear about what you stand for

**Stage 2: LIKE** - Do people enjoy you?
- Share opinions and perspective
- Use humor (authentic to you)
- Show interests and passions
- Admit mistakes and learning journey

**Stage 3: TRUST** - Are you reliable?
- Real customer testimonials
- Before/after case studies
- Behind-the-scenes showing real work
- Acknowledgment of limitations
- Transparent about constraints

*Don't expect one piece to do all three. Use different content for each stage.*

## DETECTION: IS MY COPY SOUNDING AI?

**Quick scan (read aloud):**
- Tongue trips over phrases? → Rewrite more conversationally
- Every paragraph same length? → Break pattern
- Using "furthermore," "moreover" repeatedly? → Cut 50%
- No specific examples? → Add them
- Feels emotionally flat? → Add vulnerability or personality
- Can't visualize it? → Add sensory details
- Could be about anyone? → Add personal perspective

---

## THE 5-MINUTE FIX

If your copy feels robotic:

1. **Delete 50% of transition words** (start with "furthermore," "moreover," "additionally")
2. **Add one personal story** (specific, with emotion)
3. **Replace 3 formal words** (utilize→use, facilitate→help, demonstrate→show)
4. **Break up 2 long paragraphs** into 3-4 shorter ones
5. **Read aloud once**

This often fixes the robotic feeling immediately.

## CONVERSATION STARTERS (Instead of Corporate Blah)

### CORPORATE: "It is important to understand the fundamentals..."
### HUMAN: "Here's what I wish someone had told me..."

### CORPORATE: "Our product facilitates workflow optimization..."
### HUMAN: "It saves you 2 hours a day. Here's how Sarah used it..."

### CORPORATE: "In today's fast-paced world, businesses must..."
### HUMAN: "If you're like me, you're drowning in emails..."

### CORPORATE: "The comprehensive nature of our solution..."
### HUMAN: "It handles everything—from onboarding to reporting..."

### CORPORATE: "We serve as a testament to innovation..."
### HUMAN: "We didn't plan it this way, but we ended up..."

## EMOTIONS THAT SELL (Research-Backed)

**Powerful emotions to tap:**
- Fear (of missing out, of making wrong choice)
- Relief (problem solved, anxiety gone)
- Identity (this is who I am/want to be)
- Belonging (people like me use this)
- Achievement (I'm making progress)
- Hope (better future is possible)
- Joy (experiencing delight or ease)

**Show these through story, not claims.**

---

## AUTHENTIC VULNERABILITY (Done Right)

**WORKS:** "I tried this approach 5 times and failed. Here's what I learned..."
**DOESN'T WORK:** "I'm not perfect" (when you're claiming to be the best)

**WORKS:** "This doesn't work for everyone. It works best if you..."
**DOESN'T WORK:** "Of course we have some tiny limitation..." (when it's actually major)

**WORKS:** "I was skeptical too. Here's what changed my mind..."
**DOESN'T WORK:** "Obviously everyone should want this..." (no vulnerability here)

**Principle:** Real limitations acknowledged > fake perfection claimed

## FINAL PRINCIPLE

**People don't buy products. They buy solutions to their problems and improvements to their lives.**

Humanized copy wins because it:
1. Shows you understand their specific problem
2. Proves you have a real solution
3. Demonstrates genuine care for their wellbeing
4. Respects their intelligence and autonomy
5. Includes enough personality that you feel like a real person
6. Uses their language and addresses their actual fears
7. Shows both benefits AND limitations

**In 2025:** With AI generating polished, generic copy everywhere, authentic human voice is MORE valuable, not less. Your personality and perspective are your competitive advantage.
