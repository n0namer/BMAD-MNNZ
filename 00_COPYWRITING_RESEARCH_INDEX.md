## KEY CONCEPTS ACROSS DOCUMENTS

### The 6 Core Principles (Universal)

1. **Respect audience intelligence** - Make them feel understood
2. **Conversational tone** - Vary language, use contractions
3. **Emotional connection** - Lead with emotions, facts support
4. **Specificity** - Concrete details beat abstractions
5. **Transparency** - Admit limitations
6. **Reality-grounded** - Real stories, real data, real proof

### The Know-Like-Trust Framework (Connection)

**Stage 1: KNOW** - Who are you? What do you stand for?
**Stage 2: LIKE** - Do they enjoy your personality?
**Stage 3: TRUST** - Are you reliable and competent?

### The 6 Voice Types (Context)

- Professional but warm (B2B services)
- Casual and friendly (lifestyle)
- Educational (complex products)
- Humorous (entertainment)
- Minimalist (luxury)
- Storytelling (transformation)

### The 3-Phase Implementation (Execution)

**Phase 1: Research** - Understand audience and product
**Phase 2: Draft** - Write conversationally
**Phase 3: Refine** - Add humanity and verify

### The Anti-Pattern Cluster (Detection)

- Overused phrases (294,000x more in AI)
- Perfect symmetry (too polished)
- Uniform sentence length (metronome effect)
- Missing personality (no personal footprints)
- Excessive hedging (sounds uncertain)
- Formal register (never varies)
- Absence of lived experience (too abstract)

## KEY STATISTICS

### AI Overuse Frequencies
- "As an AI language model" = 294,000x more common
- "As a powerful reminder" = 43,000x more common
- "Faced numerous challenges" = 30,000x more common
- "Provides new insights" = 22,000x more common

### Human Writing Characteristics
- Contractions: 30-50% of text
- Sentence variation: 5-30+ words (not uniform)
- Transition phrases: <1 per 3 paragraphs (not excessive)
- Personal stories: At least 1 per major section

### Research Data
- AI hedging language: Uniformly cautious throughout
- Human hedging language: Varies by evidence level
- Em-dash overuse: 2-5x more common in AI text
- Sentence length: AI maintains 12-18 words; humans vary

### Detection Accuracy
- Human expert readers: ~90% accuracy
- AI detection tools: ~80% accuracy (with 20-30% false positives)
- Non-native English: 70% false positive rate from tools
