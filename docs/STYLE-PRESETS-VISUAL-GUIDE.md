### 🎓 Educator

```
HOW TO GENERATE 80+ DOCUMENTS IN 2 HOURS: A STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

Most documentation projects follow the traditional process: plan → research →
write → edit. This takes weeks. But there's a better way.

Here's exactly how to generate comprehensive documentation in 2 hours:

**STEP 1: CAPTURE YOUR KNOWLEDGE (30 minutes)**
Instead of writing, have a conversation. Tell the system about your product:
- What does it do?
- Who uses it?
- What problems does it solve?
- What are the main features?

The system asks clarifying questions. You answer naturally. No jargon required.

**STEP 2: BUILD YOUR STRUCTURE (20 minutes)**
The system shows you a documentation structure it derived from your answers:
- Main sections
- Subsections
- Logical flow

You review and adjust: "Add this section" or "Merge these two." Within minutes,
your documentation skeleton is ready.

**STEP 3: GENERATE CONTENT (60 minutes)**
The system fills in every section with detailed, accurate content specific to
your product. You review each section (2-3 minutes per section).

**RESULT: 80+ documents in 2 hours**

Try this approach on your next project.
```

## QUICK SELECTOR TOOL

### "Which Style Should I Use?"

```
Are you teaching something?
├─ YES → Educator or Warm Expert
└─ NO → Go to next question

Are you sharing personal experience?
├─ YES → Friend Chatting or Storyteller
└─ NO → Go to next question

Are you making a contrarian argument?
├─ YES → Provocateur
└─ NO → Go to next question

Do you have data to back this up?
├─ YES → Data-Driven or Professional Authority
└─ NO → Go to next question

Are you on Twitter/need to be short?
├─ YES → Minimalist
└─ NO → Default to Warm Expert (most versatile)
```
