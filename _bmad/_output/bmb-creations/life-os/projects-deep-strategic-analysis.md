## 5. RISK ASSESSMENT & MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Strategy Doesn't Work in Live Market

**Probability:** 40-50% (many traders fail)
**Impact:** Lose $1,000-5,000 capital
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Extensive backtesting (3+ years data, multiple timeframes)
- ✅ Paper trading validation (4+ weeks)
- ✅ Gradual capital scale-up (start $5K, not $50K)
- ✅ Position sizing (max 2% risk per trade)

---

### Risk 2: Market Crash / Black Swan Event

**Probability:** 5-10%/year
**Impact:** 20-50% portfolio loss in single day
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Stop-loss orders (automatic 5% loss limit per position)
- ✅ Portfolio diversification (stocks + crypto + options)
- ✅ Never use full leverage (max 3x, not 5x+)
- ✅ Keep emergency fund ($2K separate, never trade)

---

### Risk 3: Technical Failure (Bot Crashes, Data Issue)

**Probability:** 5-10%
**Impact:** Missed trades, incorrect execution
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Redundancy (2 servers, 2 brokers)
- ✅ Monitoring alerts (email + SMS on errors)
- ✅ Weekly code reviews
- ✅ Gradual deployment (1 strategy at a time)

---

### Risk 4: Regulatory Changes

**Probability:** 10-20%
**Impact:** Restrictions on leverage or trading pairs
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Trade via regulated brokers (Alpaca, Interactive Brokers)
- ✅ Monitor regulatory updates
- ✅ Flexible strategy (can switch to different markets)

---

### Risk 5: Slippage & Execution Cost Eat Profits

**Probability:** 20-30%
**Impact:** Strategy works in backtest but loses money live
**Mitigation:**
- ✅ Model slippage in backtest (0.1-0.3% per trade)
- ✅ Limit order strategy (not market orders)
- ✅ Trade liquid instruments only
- ✅ Scale back position if profit margins thin
