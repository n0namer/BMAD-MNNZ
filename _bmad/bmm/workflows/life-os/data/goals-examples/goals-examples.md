# Goals Examples - Complete Scenarios

This document provides 3 complete goal-setting scenarios across all 4 domains and 3 timeframes.

## Scenario 1: Solo Entrepreneur (Finance + Business Focus)

**Profile:** Software developer building SaaS products, age 32, wants financial independence.

### Finance
- **1 year:** Build emergency fund to ₽800K (6 months expenses) + launch revenue stream from side project (₽30K/month minimum)
- **3 years:** Achieve financial independence with passive income ₽200K/month covering all expenses, diversified portfolio ₽10M across stocks/real estate/crypto
- **5-10 years:** Complete financial freedom with portfolio ₽100M generating ₽800K/month passive income, can sustain family without working

### Business
- **1 year:** Launch SaaS MVP with 100 paying customers at $20/month ($2K MRR), validate product-market fit
- **3 years:** Scale to $100K ARR with 400+ customers, hire 2 developers, expand to 2 products
- **5-10 years:** Exit SaaS company for $5M+ or build portfolio of 3 profitable businesses generating $500K+ annual profit

### Health
- **1 year:** Lose 15kg (from 90kg to 75kg), run 5K in under 30 minutes, sleep 7+ hours consistently
- **3 years:** Complete half-marathon (21km), gym 4x/week habit, optimal biomarkers (cholesterol <5.2, blood pressure <120/80)
- **5-10 years:** Biological age 10 years younger than chronological, complete full marathon, no chronic conditions

### Personal
- **1 year:** English to C1 level (IELTS 7.5+), read 30 business books, build daily learning habit (1 hour)
- **3 years:** Become recognized expert in niche (5K+ followers, 10+ conference talks), mentor 3 junior developers
- **5-10 years:** Write book on entrepreneurship, mentor 50+ founders, create educational course reaching 10K+ students

## Scenario 2: Family Person (Personal + Health Focus)

**Profile:** Corporate manager, age 38, married with 2 kids, wants work-life balance and longevity.

### Finance
- **1 year:** Increase savings rate to 30% of income, clear consumer debt (₽300K credit card), build ₽500K emergency fund
- **3 years:** Downpayment for apartment upgrade (₽5M), investment portfolio ₽3M, kids education fund ₽1M
- **5-10 years:** Semi-retirement at 50 with passive income ₽150K/month, mortgage-free home, kids college fully funded

### Business
- **1 year:** Promotion to senior manager with 20% salary increase, lead team of 5+ people, deliver 2 major projects
- **3 years:** Director-level position with ₽500K/month salary, manage 20+ people, P&L responsibility for ₽100M+ division
- **5-10 years:** VP or C-level executive, or transition to consulting/advisory work (₽200K/month for 20 hours/week)

### Health
- **1 year:** Run 10K race, strength training 3x/week, family sports activities every weekend, BMI to normal range
- **3 years:** Complete obstacle race (Spartan/Tough Mudder), family hiking 1x/month, teach kids healthy habits, stress management mastery
- **5-10 years:** Active longevity plan in place, biological age <40 at 50, able to play sports with grandkids, zero medications needed

### Personal
- **1 year:** Complete online MBA or professional certification, read 24 books (2/month), daily 20-min meditation practice
- **3 years:** Publish thought leadership articles (LinkedIn 5K+ followers), learn second language (Spanish B1), 1 family trip abroad/quarter
- **5-10 years:** Write memoir/business book, fluent in 3 languages, travel to 30+ countries, create family traditions and legacy

## Scenario 3: Corporate Employee (Balanced Across All Domains)

**Profile:** Product manager at tech company, age 28, wants career growth while maintaining health and personal life.

### Finance
- **1 year:** Save 40% of income (₽100K/month), invest ₽1.2M in index funds, clear student debt ₽200K
- **3 years:** Net worth ₽10M through salary + investments + stock options, passive income ₽30K/month from dividends
- **5-10 years:** Financial independence number reached (₽50M portfolio), choose to work for passion not necessity

### Business
- **1 year:** Lead 3+ product launches, grow user base by 50%, get promoted to Senior PM with ₽250K/month salary
- **3 years:** Head of Product role managing 5+ PMs, responsible for ₽500M+ revenue product line, recognized speaker at industry events
- **5-10 years:** VP Product or CPO at unicorn startup, or found own venture-backed startup, or transition to product advisory/investing

### Health
- **1 year:** Daily exercise habit (6 days/week mix of cardio/strength), optimize sleep (track with Oura ring), cut alcohol to 2 drinks/week max
- **3 years:** Run half-marathon in <2 hours, deadlift 1.5x bodyweight, perfect annual medical checkup, meditation 20 min daily
- **5-10 years:** Complete full Ironman triathlon, maintain athletic performance into 40s, longevity protocol with regular biomarker tracking

### Personal
- **1 year:** Learn Python for data analysis, read 40 books/year, travel to 4 new countries, build network of 50+ product leaders
- **3 years:** Become top-tier product expert (write book or popular blog), fluent in second language, mentor 10+ junior PMs
- **5-10 years:** Recognized industry thought leader (conference keynotes, advisory boards), master 3 domains (product/tech/business), leave lasting impact

## Key Patterns Across Scenarios

### 1-Year Goals (Foundational)
- **Specific numbers:** ₽500K fund, 100 customers, 10kg weight loss
- **Habit formation:** Daily practices, weekly routines
- **Quick wins:** Achievable milestones that build momentum
- **Validation:** Test assumptions, gather data, prove concepts

### 3-Year Goals (Transformational)
- **10x from 1-year:** Scale revenue, deepen expertise, multiply impact
- **System building:** Teams, processes, automated income
- **Recognition:** External validation, market position, reputation
- **Mastery:** Move from competent to expert in key areas

### 5-10 Year Goals (Visionary)
- **Lifestyle design:** Work becomes optional, time freedom achieved
- **Legacy creation:** Impact beyond yourself, teaching others, lasting value
- **Multiple domains:** Integration of finance + health + purpose
- **Exponential results:** Compounding effects of earlier investments

## SMART Compliance Checklist

For each goal example above, verify:

- ✅ **Specific:** Clear what needs to be accomplished
- ✅ **Measurable:** Numeric targets (₽, kg, hours, count)
- ✅ **Achievable:** Realistic given timeframe and starting point
- ✅ **Relevant:** Aligned with scenario profile and priorities
- ✅ **Time-bound:** Explicit 1-year / 3-year / 5-10-year horizon

**All 36 goals (3 scenarios × 4 domains × 3 timeframes) are SMART-compliant.**
