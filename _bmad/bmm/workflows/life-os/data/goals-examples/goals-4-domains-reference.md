# 4 Domains Deep Dive

Complete reference for setting goals across the 4 life domains.

## Domain 1: Finance (Финансы)

### What This Domain Covers
- Income: Salary, business revenue, passive income streams
- Savings: Emergency fund, specific purchases, downpayments
- Investments: Stocks, real estate, crypto, retirement accounts
- Debt: Elimination plans, interest optimization
- Net worth: Total assets minus liabilities tracking

### Example Goals by Timeframe

**1-Year (Foundational):**
- Build 3-6 month emergency fund (₽300K-₽800K)
- Increase income by 20-30% (raise, promotion, side income)
- Clear high-interest debt (credit cards, consumer loans)
- Start systematic investing (10-20% of income monthly)
- Launch first passive income source (₽10K-₽30K/month)

**3-Year (Growth):**
- Financial independence number 30% reached
- Portfolio diversification (3+ asset classes)
- Passive income covers 50%+ of expenses
- Net worth ₽5M-₽15M depending on starting point
- Multiple income streams (3+ sources)

**5-10 Year (Freedom):**
- Complete financial independence (expenses covered without working)
- Portfolio ₽30M-₽100M+ with 6-8% annual returns
- Transition from active to passive/portfolio income
- Generational wealth creation (family trust, inheritance planning)
- Exit events (business sale, IPO, major asset liquidation)

### Common Sub-Goals
- Emergency fund: 3 months → 6 months → 12 months
- Passive income: ₽10K/mo → ₽50K/mo → ₽200K/mo
- Investment portfolio: ₽500K → ₽5M → ₽50M
- Savings rate: 10% → 30% → 50%+ of income

## Domain 2: Business (Бизнес)

### What This Domain Covers
- Revenue: MRR/ARR for products, salary for employment
- Customers: Acquisition, retention, expansion
- Products: Development, launches, market fit
- Team: Hiring, management, culture
- Market position: Competitive advantage, brand recognition

### Example Goals by Timeframe

**1-Year (Validation):**
- Launch MVP or new product feature
- First 100-1000 paying customers
- ₽100K-₽1M monthly revenue milestone
- Validate product-market fit (retention >60%)
- Team expansion (first 1-3 hires)

**3-Year (Scale):**
- ₽10M-₽50M ARR depending on market
- 1K-10K customers with 70%+ retention
- Team of 10-30 people with clear structure
- Market leadership in niche (Top 3 position)
- Multiple products or revenue streams

**5-10 Year (Exit/Legacy):**
- Exit event ($5M-$50M+ valuation)
- Portfolio of multiple businesses
- Industry thought leadership and recognition
- Transition to investor/advisor/board roles
- Build category-defining company

### Example Goals by Role

**Employee/Manager:**
- 1-year: Promotion + 20-30% raise, lead team of 3-5
- 3-year: Director/VP level, manage 15-30 people, P&L responsibility
- 5-10-year: C-suite or transition to consulting/entrepreneurship

**Entrepreneur:**
- 1-year: ₽1M-₽5M revenue, product-market fit proven
- 3-year: ₽20M-₽100M revenue, profitable unit economics
- 5-10-year: Exit, IPO, or sustainable ₽100M+ business

**Freelancer/Creator:**
- 1-year: ₽200K-₽500K/month income, 20+ steady clients
- 3-year: Premium positioning, ₽1M+/month, team support
- 5-10-year: Transition to products/courses, passive ₽500K+/month

## Domain 3: Health (Здоровье)

### What This Domain Covers
- Physical fitness: Cardio, strength, flexibility, endurance
- Body composition: Weight, body fat %, muscle mass
- Biomarkers: Blood tests, blood pressure, resting heart rate
- Mental health: Stress management, meditation, therapy
- Longevity: Biological age, healthspan, preventive care
- Habits: Sleep, nutrition, exercise consistency

### Example Goals by Timeframe

**1-Year (Foundation):**
- Weight management: Lose/gain 5-15kg to optimal range
- Fitness milestone: Run 5K, 10K, or first gym program completion
- Habit formation: Exercise 3-4x/week consistently
- Sleep optimization: 7-8 hours nightly with tracking
- Quit/reduce: Smoking, alcohol, processed foods

**3-Year (Mastery):**
- Athletic achievement: Half-marathon, obstacle race, bodyweight 1.5x deadlift
- Optimal biomarkers: All blood work in healthy ranges
- Daily practice: Meditation, yoga, or mobility work
- Stress management: Measurable HRV improvement, low cortisol
- Advanced tracking: Continuous glucose monitor, Oura ring data

**5-10 Year (Longevity):**
- Biological age <chronological age by 5-10 years
- Major endurance event: Marathon, Ironman, ultra-distance
- Disease prevention: Zero chronic conditions, no medications
- Family health: Influence kids/partner, create healthy legacy
- Longevity protocol: Regular testing, supplementation, optimization

### Common Metrics
- Cardio: 5K time → 10K time → half-marathon → marathon
- Strength: Bodyweight exercises → 1x bodyweight lifts → 1.5-2x bodyweight
- Body composition: BMI normal → body fat 15-20% → body fat 10-15%
- Resting heart rate: 70 bpm → 60 bpm → 50 bpm (athlete level)

## Domain 4: Personal Development (Личное развитие)

### What This Domain Covers
- Skills: Technical, creative, interpersonal competencies
- Knowledge: Reading, courses, certifications, degrees
- Relationships: Family, friends, network, mentors
- Experiences: Travel, hobbies, adventures, cultural exposure
- Identity: Values, purpose, self-awareness, personal brand
- Contribution: Teaching, mentoring, legacy projects

### Example Goals by Timeframe

**1-Year (Skill Building):**
- Learn new skill: Language (A2-B1), coding, design, marketing
- Reading habit: 20-50 books (1-4 per month)
- Network expansion: Attend 6+ events, build 30+ meaningful connections
- Daily learning: 30-60 minutes deliberate practice
- Online presence: Start blog, YouTube, or LinkedIn content

**3-Year (Expertise):**
- Master 1-2 domains: Recognized expert, speaking opportunities
- Language fluency: B2-C1 in second language
- Certification/degree: MBA, professional credentials, specialized training
- Thought leadership: 1K-10K followers, regular content creation
- Mentor others: Guide 5-10 people in your expertise area

**5-10 Year (Legacy):**
- Polymath: 2-3 areas of deep expertise
- Industry recognition: Conference keynotes, book authorship, advisory boards
- Massive mentorship: Impact 100+ people directly
- Create lasting work: Course, book, nonprofit, movement
- Values alignment: Life fully consistent with core principles

### Common Pathways
- **Language:** A1 (basics) → B1 (conversational) → C1 (fluent)
- **Expertise:** Novice → Competent → Expert → Thought leader
- **Content:** 0 followers → 1K → 10K → 100K+
- **Teaching:** Learn → Do → Teach → Mentor → Systematize

## Balancing Across Domains

### Anti-Patterns (Avoid)
- All goals in 1-2 domains, ignoring others
- Finance/Business at expense of Health/Personal
- 10-year vision with no 1-year foundation
- All goals vague or all hyper-specific (need mix)

### Healthy Patterns
- At least 2 goals per domain (12 total minimum)
- 1-year goals support 3-year goals support 5-10-year vision
- Cascading impact: Business success → Financial freedom → Time for Health/Personal
- Integrated lifestyle: Work and life goals reinforce each other

### Conflict Resolution

**Common Conflicts:**
1. Business growth vs Health (100-hour weeks sacrifice fitness)
2. Finance accumulation vs Personal experiences (save vs travel)
3. Career focus vs Relationships (promotion requires relocation)

**Resolution Strategies:**
- Sequence: Focus on Business 1-3 years, then Health 3-5 years
- Integration: Build health into work (standing desk, walking meetings)
- Trade-offs: Explicit choices with awareness (choose career now, family later)
- Non-negotiables: Define minimums (e.g., always 3x/week exercise, no matter what)

## Domain Interdependencies

### Positive Cycles
- **Health → Business:** More energy, focus, decision quality → better performance
- **Business → Finance:** Revenue growth → investment capacity → passive income
- **Personal → Business:** Skills/network → opportunities → career acceleration
- **Finance → Personal:** Resources → time/freedom → pursue learning/experiences

### Negative Spirals (to avoid)
- Poor health → low productivity → business struggles → financial stress → worse health
- Neglected relationships → isolation → motivation drop → all goals suffer
- Financial pressure → accept any work → no skill building → career stagnation

**Strategy:** Break negative spirals by investing in foundation (Health, Personal) even when urgent (Finance, Business) demands attention.
