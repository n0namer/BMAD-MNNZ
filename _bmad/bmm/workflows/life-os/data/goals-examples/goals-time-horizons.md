# Time Horizons - 1 Year / 3 Years / 5-10 Years

Guide to setting goals across different time horizons and cascading from long-term vision to short-term execution.

## Why 3 Time Horizons?

### 1-Year Goals: Foundation & Execution
- **Purpose:** Concrete, measurable achievements you can complete in 12 months
- **Characteristics:** Specific actions, clear metrics, high certainty
- **Examples:** Launch product, lose 10kg, read 30 books, save ₽500K
- **Failure rate:** Low if well-planned (70-80% completion typical)

### 3-Year Goals: Transformation
- **Purpose:** Ambitious outcomes requiring sustained effort and skill development
- **Characteristics:** Bigger scale (10x from 1-year), system building, compounding effects
- **Examples:** Grow to ₽10M ARR, run marathon, become recognized expert, achieve ₽10M net worth
- **Failure rate:** Medium (40-60% completion due to pivots/life changes)

### 5-10 Year Goals: Vision
- **Purpose:** Directional guidance, lifestyle design, legacy creation
- **Characteristics:** Inspirational, flexible, may shift as you grow
- **Examples:** Financial independence, industry leadership, biological age reversal, generational impact
- **Failure rate:** High (30-40% completion), but serves as North Star even if adjusted

**Key Insight:** Longer horizons have higher uncertainty but provide crucial direction. Shorter horizons have higher certainty but need alignment with long-term vision.

## 1-Year Goals Deep Dive

### Characteristics of Great 1-Year Goals

**Must be:**
- Specific enough to start working on next week
- Measurable with monthly progress tracking
- Achievable with available resources and realistic effort
- Connected to 3-year goal as foundational step

**Avoid:**
- Vague aspirations ("be healthier")
- Overly ambitious relative to starting point (0 to ₽100M revenue)
- Dependent entirely on external factors (luck, others' decisions)
- Disconnected from longer-term vision

### Example 1-Year Goals by Domain

**Finance:**
- Build ₽500K emergency fund (6 months expenses) by saving ₽42K/month
- Launch passive income stream generating ₽30K/month minimum by Q4
- Increase salary from ₽200K to ₽250K through promotion or job change
- Clear ₽300K consumer debt through aggressive repayment plan

**Business:**
- Launch SaaS MVP with 100 paying customers at ₽2,000/month each (₽200K MRR)
- Grow agency from ₽500K to ₽1.5M annual revenue (3x) with 80%+ client retention
- Publish 50 blog posts and grow email list from 0 to 2,000 subscribers
- Complete first product launch generating ₽1M in first-year sales

**Health:**
- Lose 12kg (from 85kg to 73kg) through 4x/week gym and nutrition tracking
- Run first 10K race in under 60 minutes by training 3x/week
- Achieve 7+ hours sleep consistently (90% of nights) through better sleep hygiene
- Reduce body fat from 25% to 18% while maintaining muscle mass

**Personal:**
- Achieve English B2 level (IELTS 6.5+) through daily 1-hour practice
- Read 36 books (3/month) covering business, psychology, and biography
- Attend 12 networking events and build relationships with 30 founders
- Complete online course (e.g., CS50, Deep Learning Specialization) with 90%+ scores

### Quarterly Breakdown

**Q1 (Jan-Mar):** Foundation
- Set up systems, habits, infrastructure
- Early wins to build momentum
- Learn foundational skills
- **Example:** Set up savings automation, join gym, enroll in course

**Q2 (Apr-Jun):** Acceleration
- Scale up effort based on Q1 learnings
- Hit first major milestones
- Optimize what's working, drop what's not
- **Example:** Launch MVP, hit 5kg loss milestone, complete course modules 1-3

**Q3 (Jul-Sep):** Optimization
- Refine processes, improve efficiency
- Course-correct based on data
- Push through "messy middle"
- **Example:** Iterate product based on feedback, dial in nutrition, apply learnings to projects

**Q4 (Oct-Dec):** Sprint to finish
- Final push to hit annual goals
- Prepare foundation for next year
- Reflect and plan ahead
- **Example:** Reach 100 customers, hit 12kg loss target, complete course certification

## 3-Year Goals Deep Dive

### Characteristics of Great 3-Year Goals

**Should be:**
- 10x bigger impact than 1-year goal (revenue, scale, expertise)
- Require system building (team, processes, automation)
- Include external recognition or validation (customers, market, credentials)
- Demonstrate mastery or leadership in chosen area

**Transformation markers:**
- From doer → manager/leader
- From salary → diversified income
- From beginner → expert
- From local → regional/national
- From idea → proven business model

### Example 3-Year Goals by Domain

**Finance:**
- Achieve ₽15M net worth through business equity + investment portfolio
- Passive income of ₽150K/month covering 100% of lifestyle expenses (financial independence)
- Diversified portfolio across 3+ asset classes (stocks, real estate, business equity)
- Multiple income streams (3-5 sources) with no single source >50% of total

**Business:**
- Scale to ₽50M ARR with 80%+ gross margins and profitability
- Build team of 20+ people with documented processes and culture
- Achieve market leadership in niche (Top 3 position, recognized brand)
- Successfully launch 2-3 product lines with proven economics

**Health:**
- Complete full marathon in under 4 hours after running 3 half-marathons
- Maintain 15% body fat year-round with visible abs and athletic physique
- All biomarkers in optimal range (not just "normal"): cholesterol <5.0, HbA1c <5.5, BP <120/80
- Daily meditation/mindfulness practice (365-day streak) with measurable stress reduction

**Personal:**
- Become recognized expert: 10+ conference talks, 5,000+ followers, published articles
- Fluent in second language (C1 level) with ability to work/present in that language
- Complete advanced degree or prestigious certification (MBA, professional designation)
- Mentor 10+ people with documented impact on their careers/lives

### Year-by-Year Progression

**Year 1:** Validation & Foundation
- Prove the concept works
- Build initial customer base/audience/expertise
- Establish habits and systems
- **Milestone:** ₽1M-₽5M revenue, first 100 customers, first certification

**Year 2:** Scale & Optimization
- 3-5x growth from Year 1
- Hire first team members
- Refine processes and playbooks
- **Milestone:** ₽5M-₽20M revenue, 500 customers, thought leadership emerging

**Year 3:** Market Position & Mastery
- 2-3x growth from Year 2
- Clear competitive advantage
- External recognition and validation
- **Milestone:** ₽20M-₽50M revenue, 1,000+ customers, industry expert status

## 5-10 Year Goals Deep Dive

### Characteristics of Great 5-10 Year Goals

**Vision-oriented:**
- Describe desired lifestyle, not just achievements
- Allow flexibility in HOW you get there
- Focus on identity and legacy, not just metrics
- Paint picture of "day in the life" at destination

**Common themes:**
- Financial freedom (work becomes optional)
- Expertise and influence (thought leader, advisor, investor)
- Health and longevity (biological age younger than chronological)
- Impact and legacy (teaching, mentoring, building lasting value)

### Example 5-10 Year Goals by Domain

**Finance:**
- Complete financial independence: ₽100M portfolio generating ₽8M/year passive income (6-8% returns)
- Work becomes optional: Can sustain lifestyle for 30+ years without working
- Generational wealth: Family trust, estate plan, kids' futures secured
- Freedom to pursue passion projects without income requirement

**Business:**
- Exit/IPO event: Sell company for ₽500M-₽5B or take public
- Portfolio approach: Own equity in 5+ businesses, angel investor in 20+
- Industry shaper: Recognized globally, advisory boards, keynote speaker
- Transition to investor/mentor: Help next generation of entrepreneurs

**Health:**
- Biological age 10-15 years younger than chronological (50 years old, biological 35-40)
- Athletic achievements: Ironman triathlon, ultra-marathon, or equivalent
- Zero chronic conditions: No regular medications, optimal health into 60s-70s
- Active longevity: Able to play sports with grandchildren, travel extensively

**Personal:**
- Polymathic mastery: Deep expertise in 3+ different domains
- Legacy creation: Published book, established course, nonprofit/movement founded
- Massive mentorship: Directly impacted 100+ people, indirectly 10,000+
- Values alignment: Life fully expresses core values, zero compromises

### Backward Planning from Vision

**Start with destination (Year 10):**
- Describe ideal day: Where are you? What are you doing? Who are you with?
- Key outcomes: What have you built/achieved/become?
- Identity: How do others describe you? What are you known for?

**Work backward to milestones:**
- Year 7-10: Harvest phase (enjoying fruits of earlier work)
- Year 4-6: Growth phase (scaling what works, pivoting from what doesn't)
- Year 1-3: Foundation phase (experimenting, learning, building)

**Example:**
- **Year 10 vision:** Financially independent with ₽100M portfolio, advising 10 companies, living 6 months/year traveling
- **Year 6 milestone:** ₽30M net worth, exited first company, portfolio of investments established
- **Year 3 milestone:** ₽10M net worth, company at ₽50M ARR, angel investing begun
- **Year 1 milestone:** ₽2M net worth, company at ₽5M ARR, first angel investment made

## Cascading Goals (Top-Down Alignment)

### The Cascade Process

**Step 1: Define 5-10 year vision**
- Start with desired lifestyle and identity
- Be specific about freedom, impact, health, relationships
- Write narrative description, not just bullet points

**Step 2: Reverse-engineer 3-year milestone**
- What position must you be in at Year 3 to reach Year 10 vision?
- Typically: 1/3 to 1/2 of the way to ultimate goal
- More concrete and measurable than 10-year vision

**Step 3: Determine 1-year foundation**
- What must you accomplish in next 12 months to be on track for 3-year milestone?
- Highly specific, actionable, weekly/monthly trackable
- Should feel challenging but definitely achievable

### Cascade Example 1: Financial Independence

**10-Year:** Financial independence with ₽100M portfolio generating ₽8M/year passive income

**3-Year:** ₽30M net worth with ₽2.5M annual passive income from diversified sources

**1-Year:** ₽3M net worth milestone through business growth (₽2M equity) + investments (₽1M portfolio)

**This quarter:** Grow business from ₽5M to ₽7M ARR (₽500K equity increase) + invest ₽150K into index funds

**This month:** Ship 2 product features, acquire 20 new customers at ₽3K/month each, invest ₽50K

### Cascade Example 2: Business Scale

**10-Year:** Exit for ₽1B+ valuation, become venture investor and advisor

**3-Year:** ₽200M ARR SaaS business, clear market leader, Series B raised

**1-Year:** ₽20M ARR with 80% retention, product-market fit proven, 50-person team

**This quarter:** Grow from ₽15M to ₽18M ARR, ship enterprise features, hire 5 key people

**This month:** Close 10 new enterprise deals, ship SSO and RBAC features, make 2 offers

### Cascade Example 3: Health & Longevity

**10-Year:** Biological age 40 at chronological 55, Ironman finisher, zero medications

**3-Year:** Biological age 35 at chronological 42, marathon finisher, all biomarkers optimal

**1-Year:** Biological age 37 at chronological 39, half-marathon finisher, BMI 22

**This quarter:** Lose 3kg (from 78kg to 75kg), run 10K in <55min, sleep 7+ hours 90% of nights

**This month:** 12 runs totaling 60km, strength train 8 sessions, track sleep and hit 7+ hours 25+ nights

## Common Mistakes & Fixes

### Mistake 1: Disconnected goals
**Problem:** 1-year goal doesn't build toward 3-year, 3-year doesn't build toward 10-year
**Fix:** For each goal, ask "How does this enable the next level?" If unclear, realign.

### Mistake 2: All short-term, no vision
**Problem:** Only 1-year goals, no longer-term direction
**Fix:** Risk of tactical success but strategic failure. Add 3-year and 10-year goals for direction.

### Mistake 3: All vision, no execution
**Problem:** Grand 10-year dreams but no 1-year concrete steps
**Fix:** Won't make progress. Break vision into specific 1-year foundational goals.

### Mistake 4: Unrealistic cascading
**Problem:** 1-year goal is ₽1M revenue, 3-year goal is ₽100M revenue (100x in 2 years)
**Fix:** Make progression realistic (e.g., ₽1M → ₽5M → ₽20M → ₽100M over 10 years).

### Mistake 5: Rigid long-term goals
**Problem:** Treating 10-year goals as fixed commitments rather than directional guidance
**Fix:** Review annually, adjust based on new information. Vision evolves as you grow.

## Quick Reference Table

| Horizon | Purpose | Characteristics | Review Frequency | Adjustment Flexibility |
|---------|---------|----------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **1-Year** | Execution | Specific, measurable, certain | Weekly/Monthly | Low (commit and deliver) |
| **3-Year** | Transformation | Ambitious, system-building | Quarterly | Medium (adjust based on results) |
| **5-10-Year** | Vision | Inspirational, directional | Annually | High (evolve as you grow) |

**Usage:** Start with 10-year vision, cascade to 3-year milestone, then define 1-year foundation. Review and adjust top-down quarterly.
