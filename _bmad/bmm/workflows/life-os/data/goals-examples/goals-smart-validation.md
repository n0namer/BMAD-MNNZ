# SMART Goal Validation Guide

Complete reference for validating goals against SMART criteria.

## SMART Criteria Explained

### S - Specific (Конкретная)

**What it means:** The goal is clear, unambiguous, and well-defined. Anyone reading it understands exactly what needs to be accomplished.

**Good Examples:**
- "Launch SaaS product with user authentication, payment integration, and 3 core features"
- "Run 10K race in under 50 minutes"
- "Achieve IELTS score of 7.5+ (C1 level English)"
- "Build emergency fund of ₽500,000 in high-yield savings account"

**Bad Examples (too vague):**
- "Improve business" → What aspect? How much? Which metric?
- "Get healthier" → Lose weight? Gain muscle? Better sleep? All of above?
- "Learn programming" → Which language? To what level? For what purpose?
- "Make more money" → From what? By how much? Active or passive?

**Fix Strategy:** Add WHO, WHAT, WHERE, WHEN, WHY details.

---

### M - Measurable (Измеримая)

**What it means:** The goal has quantifiable criteria for tracking progress and determining completion.

**Good Examples:**
- "Increase MRR from ₽50K to ₽200K (4x growth)"
- "Lose 12kg (from 85kg to 73kg)"
- "Read 36 books (3 per month)"
- "Grow email list from 500 to 5,000 subscribers (10x)"

**Bad Examples (not measurable):**
- "Significantly grow revenue" → How much is significant? 10%? 100%?
- "Get much better at design" → What's the metric? Portfolio pieces? Client feedback score?
- "Build stronger relationships" → How to measure? Time spent? Quality of interactions?
- "Become more productive" → Tasks completed? Revenue per hour? Subjective feeling?

**Metrics to Add:**

| Goal Type | Measurable Metric |
|-----------|------------------|
| Revenue | Specific ₽ amount, MRR, ARR |
| Customers | Count, retention %, NPS score |
| Health | kg, km, hours, reps, biomarker value |
| Skills | Test score, certification, project count |
| Audience | Follower count, engagement rate |
| Time | Hours per week/day, specific schedule |

---

### A - Achievable (Достижимая)

**What it means:** The goal is realistic given your starting point, resources, constraints, and timeframe. Ambitious but not impossible.

**Reality Check Questions:**
1. Have others with similar starting conditions achieved this?
2. Do I have or can I acquire the necessary resources (time, money, skills)?
3. Are there any insurmountable obstacles?
4. Is the timeline realistic given typical progression rates?

**Achievable Examples:**

| Starting Point | 1-Year Achievable | 1-Year Unrealistic |
|---------------|------------------|-------------------|
| ₽100K MRR business | ₽300K MRR (3x) | ₽10M MRR (100x) |
| Never run before | Complete 5K race | Run marathon in <3 hours |
| A2 English level | Reach B2 level | Native-level fluency (C2) |
| ₽200K/month salary | ₽300K/month (50% raise) | ₽1M/month (5x) |

**Warning Signs of Unachievable:**
- Requires resources you don't have and can't get (e.g., ₽50M investment with no investor access)
- Needs skills that take 5+ years to develop (e.g., become world-class surgeon in 2 years with no medical training)
- Defies typical progression rates (e.g., 0 to ₽100M revenue in 1 year as solo founder)
- Depends entirely on luck/factors outside your control (e.g., win lottery, go viral)

**How to Fix Unachievable Goals:**
1. Extend timeline (1-year → 3-year)
2. Reduce magnitude (10x → 3x)
3. Add intermediate milestones (break into smaller goals)
4. Change approach (if path is blocked, find alternative route)

---

### R - Relevant (Значимая)

**What it means:** The goal aligns with your broader objectives, values, and long-term vision. It matters to YOU, not just sounds impressive.

**Relevance Check Questions:**
1. Why does this goal matter to me personally?
2. How does achieving this support my 3-year or 5-10-year vision?
3. What happens if I DON'T achieve this? (If answer is "nothing," it's not relevant)
4. Is this MY goal or someone else's expectation?

**Relevant Examples:**

**Scenario:** Solo entrepreneur focused on financial independence.

| Relevant | Not Relevant |
|----------|-------------|
| "Build passive income to ₽200K/month" (directly enables FI) | "Get promoted to VP at company" (corporate ladder irrelevant to entrepreneur) |
| "Launch 2nd SaaS product for diversification" (reduces risk) | "Learn Mandarin Chinese" (no business/personal need for it) |
| "Optimize business to 20 hours/week" (frees time) | "Grow team to 50 people" (contradicts solo/freedom goal) |

**Anti-Pattern:** Chasing goals because they're popular/trendy, not because they align with YOUR vision.
- "Raise VC funding" when you want lifestyle business (VC requires growth-at-all-costs)
- "Build personal brand to 100K followers" when you're introverted and hate social media
- "Run marathon" when you hate running and prefer cycling (pick sport you enjoy)

---

### T - Time-bound (Ограниченная во времени)

**What it means:** The goal has a clear deadline or timeframe, creating urgency and enabling progress tracking.

**Good Examples:**
- "Launch MVP by March 31, 2026"
- "Achieve ₽500K emergency fund by December 31, 2026 (12 months)"
- "Complete half-marathon by September 15, 2026 (6 months of training)"
- "Reach 1,000 email subscribers by end of Q2 2026"

**Bad Examples (no deadline):**
- "Eventually launch a product"
- "Someday run a marathon"
- "Get fluent in English when I have time"

**Timeframe Guidelines:**

| Timeframe | Suitable For | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| 1-3 months | Habit formation, small projects | Build daily meditation habit, ship small feature |
| 3-6 months | Skill acquisition, fitness milestones | Learn React basics, run first 10K |
| 6-12 months | Product launches, major achievements | Launch MVP, achieve B2 language level |
| 1-3 years | Scale, mastery, transformation | Grow to ₽10M ARR, become recognized expert |
| 5-10 years | Vision, legacy, financial freedom | Exit for $10M, achieve complete FI |

**Deadline Pressure:**
- Too tight → Unrealistic, leads to burnout
- Too loose → No urgency, procrastination
- Just right → Challenging but achievable with focused effort

## 10 Validation Pairs (Good vs Bad)

### 1. Revenue Goal
- ❌ **Bad:** "Grow the business"
- ✅ **Good:** "Increase MRR from ₽100K to ₽300K by Dec 31, 2026 through 200 new customers"

### 2. Weight Loss Goal
- ❌ **Bad:** "Lose some weight"
- ✅ **Good:** "Lose 10kg (from 80kg to 70kg) by June 30, 2026 through 3x/week gym and calorie tracking"

### 3. Learning Goal
- ❌ **Bad:** "Get better at marketing"
- ✅ **Good:** "Complete Google Analytics + Facebook Ads certifications and run 3 profitable ad campaigns by March 31, 2026"

### 4. Income Goal
- ❌ **Bad:** "Make more money"
- ✅ **Good:** "Launch passive income stream generating ₽50K/month through info products by September 30, 2026"

### 5. Fitness Goal
- ❌ **Bad:** "Get in shape"
- ✅ **Good:** "Run 10K in under 55 minutes and deadlift 100kg by April 30, 2026"

### 6. Customer Goal
- ❌ **Bad:** "Acquire more users"
- ✅ **Good:** "Grow from 50 to 500 paying customers (10x) with >70% monthly retention by Dec 31, 2026"

### 7. Language Goal
- ❌ **Bad:** "Improve English"
- ✅ **Good:** "Achieve IELTS score 7.5+ (C1 level) by August 1, 2026 through daily 1-hour practice"

### 8. Savings Goal
- ❌ **Bad:** "Save more money"
- ✅ **Good:** "Build emergency fund of ₽600K (6 months expenses) by October 31, 2026 by saving ₽50K/month"

### 9. Productivity Goal
- ❌ **Bad:** "Be more productive"
- ✅ **Good:** "Ship 12 product features (1 per month) while working max 40 hours/week by Dec 31, 2026"

### 10. Network Goal
- ❌ **Bad:** "Meet more people"
- ✅ **Good:** "Build network of 30 founders through 6 events + 30 coffee chats by June 30, 2026"

## Validation Checklist (15 Questions)

For each goal, answer YES to all questions:

### Specific
- [ ] Can I explain this goal to someone else in one sentence?
- [ ] Is it clear what "done" looks like?
- [ ] Have I specified WHO, WHAT, WHERE, WHEN?

### Measurable
- [ ] Can I track progress with a number (₽, kg, count, %)?
- [ ] Will I know for certain when I've achieved it?
- [ ] Can I measure this weekly or monthly?

### Achievable
- [ ] Have others achieved similar results in this timeframe?
- [ ] Do I have or can I acquire necessary resources?
- [ ] Is this ambitious but realistic given my constraints?

### Relevant
- [ ] Does this goal directly support my 3-year or 10-year vision?
- [ ] Will achieving this materially improve my life?
- [ ] Is this MY goal, not someone else's expectation?

### Time-bound
- [ ] Have I set a specific deadline (day/month/year)?
- [ ] Is the timeline challenging but achievable?
- [ ] Can I break this into quarterly or monthly milestones?

**Scoring:**
- 15/15 YES → Excellent SMART goal
- 12-14 YES → Good, minor refinements needed
- 9-11 YES → Needs significant improvement
- <9 YES → Rewrite the goal from scratch

## Common Fixes

| Problem | Diagnostic | Fix |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Too vague | Fails Specific test | Add WHO, WHAT, WHERE, WHEN, WHY details |
| Can't track | Fails Measurable test | Add number (₽, kg, count, %) and measurement method |
| Impossible | Fails Achievable test | Extend timeline, reduce magnitude, or add resources |
| Doesn't matter | Fails Relevant test | Connect to bigger vision or drop the goal |
| No urgency | Fails Time-bound test | Set specific deadline with quarterly milestones |

**Master Fix Strategy:** Start with WHAT and BY WHEN, then add details until it passes all 15 checklist questions.
