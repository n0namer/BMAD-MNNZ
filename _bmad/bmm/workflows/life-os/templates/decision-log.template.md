## Examples: Decision Log Variations

### Example 1: GO/NO-GO Decision (Activate Project)

**Decision:** Activate PROJ-2025-007 (Health Habit System)

**Options Considered:**
- Option 1: Launch now with full 40-hour estimate (Chosen)
- Option 2: Scale back to 20-hour MVP version
- Option 3: Defer 3 months until Q2

**Chosen:** Launch with full scope

**Rationale:** Health priority is highest in current quarter; required resources are available; alignment with GOAL-2025-001 (wellness); delay would lose momentum and seasonal relevance

**Expected Impact:**
- Positive: Establish daily tracking habit, improve health metrics by 15-20%, ripple benefits to energy and productivity
- Risk: Time commitment might exceed 40 hours if scope creep occurs; requires sustained daily effort

**Result (3 months later):** Activated successfully; habit established; 18% improvement in health metrics; delivered in 38 hours (under estimate); became foundation for related projects

---

### Example 2: PIVOT Decision (Change Approach)

**Decision:** Pivot from Monthly Retrospectives to Weekly Quick Reviews

**Options Considered:**
- Option 1: Continue monthly 2-hour deep retrospectives
- Option 2: Switch to 15-minute weekly check-ins (Chosen)
- Option 3: Hybrid: bi-weekly combination

**Chosen:** Weekly quick reviews (15 min each)

**Rationale:** Monthly cadence was too long; issues weren't being captured timely; weekly frequency allows faster course correction; 15 min keeps barrier low and maintains consistency

**Expected Impact:**
- Positive: Better issue detection, faster feedback loops, higher completion rate of reviews
- Risk: Less deep reflection per session; might miss strategic patterns

**Result (8 weeks later):** Excellent decision; review completion rate increased from 60% to 95%; issues now caught in week 1 instead of month 2; discovered patterns faster; monthly summaries now built on weekly data instead of memory

---

### Example 3: KILL Decision (Terminate Project)

**Decision:** Kill PROJ-2024-042 (Content Archive System)

**Options Considered:**
- Option 1: Continue with original plan (60 more hours)
- Option 2: Reduce scope and complete MVP (20 more hours)
- Option 3: Kill project entirely; use alternative system (Chosen)

**Chosen:** Kill project; adopt off-the-shelf tool instead

**Rationale:** Project had consumed 80 hours with no clear end; discovered that Notion templates solve 90% of requirements at 1/10th the cost; team consensus that custom solution wasn't worth the opportunity cost; better to focus on 5 active projects

**Expected Impact:**
- Positive: Free 20 hours of project time; immediate system available; lower maintenance burden
- Risk: Migration effort; loss of custom features; team morale if seen as "failure"

**Result (6 weeks later):** Good decision overall; migration took 4 hours less than estimate; team adopted tool quickly; identified 2 custom features worth keeping in a small script (4 hours) rather than full system; freed capacity enabled PROJ-2025-001 activation 4 weeks earlier than planned
