PATTERNS THAT WORKED BEST
================================================================================

1. HIERARCHICAL SWARM TOPOLOGY
   Why: Coordinator validates each phase output against goal
   Result: Zero conflicts across 4 phases with 9 agents
   Applies To: Any phase-dependent work with 6-12 agents

2. PHASE GATING
   Why: Enforces sequential dependency order
   Result: 127 blockers executed without circular conflicts
   Applies To: Projects with >20 parallel items

3. VALIDATION-FIRST APPROACH
   Why: Catches real gaps before claiming completion
   Result: 62% to 100% through systematic escalation
   Applies To: All projects (especially high-stakes work)

4. MEMORY COORDINATION
   Why: Enables team scale without coordination overhead
   Result: 9 agents coordinated seamlessly via global memory
   Applies To: Teams of 9+ agents

5. BLOCKER ESCALATION
   Why: Systematically converts gaps to specifications
   Result: 49 gaps to 49 complete specifications
   Applies To: Projects with identified gaps
