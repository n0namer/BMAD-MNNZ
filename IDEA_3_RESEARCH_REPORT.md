## Angles Breakdown

### Top 3 Angles (Priority Order)

#### 1. **От одного человека к системе** (From Person to System)
- **Relevance:** 93/100
- **Hook:** "Не ты — твой процесс работает" (It's not you, your process works)
- **Sources:** 4
- **Why It Works:** Universal pain point - every founder hits a productivity ceiling
- **Key Insight:** Documentation > hiring. Build systems before expanding people
- **Content Angles:**
  - Before/after schedule comparison
  - Documentation ROI calculation
  - Checklist-to-process framework

#### 2. **Аутсорс ошибок вместо аутсорса работы** (Outsource Errors, Not Work)
- **Relevance:** 89/100
- **Hook:** "Найми дешевле, систематизируй жестче" (Hire cheaper, systematize harder)
- **Sources:** 3
- **Why It Works:** Most contrarian angle - reframes "cheap labor" as strategic advantage
- **Key Insight:** Systems > seniority. A junior with clear SOP > senior with chaos
- **Content Angles:**
  - Cost analysis: 1 senior ($100k) vs 3 juniors ($60k) with systems
  - SOP library as business asset
  - Quality gates at different stages

#### 3. **Профиль идеального первого нанятого** (First Hire Profile)
- **Relevance:** 86/100
- **Hook:** "Твой первый наемный - это не специалист, это твой отражение" (Your first hire isn't a specialist, it's your reflection)
- **Sources:** 4
- **Why It Works:** Actionable and urgent - first-time founders agonize over this decision
- **Key Insight:** Attitude and systems thinking > skills and experience
- **Content Angles:**
  - Attitude checklist vs. skill checklist
  - Interview questions revealing systems thinking
  - Red flags in candidate responses

## Key Research Insights

### Contrarian Framing
- **Angle a2 is the unique differentiator:** Reframes "hiring cheap labor" as a smart strategy when paired with systems. Most content says "you need senior talent"; this says "junior + systems = better ROI."

### Psychological Hooks
- **Autonomy** (a1): "Your business works without you"
- **Cost/Leverage** (a2): "Do more with less"
- **Trust/Identity** (a6): "Who you hire defines who you are"
- **Control/Standards** (a4): "Quality without sacrifice"

### Content Differentiation Strategy
**Frame all angles around Systems > People**
- Not: "How to hire better people"
- But: "How systems make any person better"
