# Classifieds Marketplace User Research - 2024/2026

## Executive Summary
Research conducted February 2026 on user pain points, fraud scenarios, and platform behavior in classifieds marketplaces. Data sourced from industry reports, marketplace platforms, and security studies.

---

## SELLER PAIN POINTS

### 1. High Return Rates & Policy Issues
- **Severity**: HIGH
- **Impact**: 20-30% average return rate
- **Fashion category**: Highest returns (30-day windows)
- **Financial loss**: Significant, especially for small sellers
- **Source**: Bill Gosling research

### 2. Buyer Fraud & Abuse
- **Severity**: CRITICAL
- **Patterns**: Fake/damaged product returns
- **Impact**: Some sellers stopping online sales entirely
- **Trust erosion**: Major contributor to seller churn
- **Trend**: Increasing cases year-over-year

### 3. Pricing Challenges
- **Severity**: MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Issues**: Market saturation (44% users on multiple platforms)
- **Competition**: Downward price pressure
- **Differentiation**: Hard to stand out in crowded marketplace
- **Need**: AI-powered pricing assistants gaining traction
- **Source**: Directorist, FasterCapital research

### 4. Listing Visibility & Competition
- **Severity**: HIGH
- **Challenge**: Sheer volume makes individual ads hard to find
- **Market saturation**: 44% of users active on multiple platforms
- **CAC increase**: 36% higher customer acquisition costs
- **Quality control**: Mix of high/low quality listings reduces trust
- **Source**: Medium, Directorist research

### 5. Fraud Risk Perception
- **Severity**: HIGH
- **Trust deficit**: 31% among first-time sellers
- **Market stability**: Constrains growth at 34% fraud risk perception
- **Platform reputation**: Key concern for onboarding
- **Source**: Market Growth Reports

### 6. Limited Customer Reviews
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **eBay**: 1 in 3 buyers leave reviews
- **Amazon**: 1 in 30 buyers leave reviews
- **Impact**: Harder to build seller reputation
- **Trust building**: Significantly slower

### 7. High Commission Fees
- **Severity**: MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Impact**: Deterrent to onboarding
- **Customization**: Limited store personalization options
- **Profitability**: Margins compressed by fees
- **Source**: Bill Gosling, Quora seller discussions

## BUYER PAIN POINTS

### 1. Complex Checkout Processes
- **Severity**: HIGH
- **Abandonment**: 83% mobile cart abandonment (vs 67% desktop)
- **Friction**: Too many fields, lengthy processes
- **Completion**: Only stubborn customers finish
- **Platform**: Worse on mobile
- **Source**: ProProfs Desk, SQ Magazine research

### 2. Trust & Verification Issues
- **Severity**: CRITICAL
- **Counterfeit products**: Major concern
  - Snapdeal: 12% received fake products
  - Amazon: 11% received fake products
  - Flipkart: 6% received fake products
- **Fraud detection**: E-commerce has 1.8x more authorized fraud than financial services
- **Identity verification**: Key need for buyer confidence
- **Source**: Local Circles survey, Veriff Fraud Report 2024

### 3. Limited Payment Options
- **Severity**: MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Impact**: 56% would buy more with more payment options
- **Security concerns**: P2P apps (Zelle, Venmo, CashApp) lack fraud protection
- **Irreversible transactions**: Major risk factor
- **Source**: OyeLabs, LifeLock research

### 4. Poor Customer Support
- **Severity**: MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Chatbot dissatisfaction**: Major population uncomfortable with automated responses
- **Resolution time**: Slow manual review processes
- **Escalation**: Limited human support access
- **Source**: Core DNA customer research

### 5. Return Policy Complexity
- **Severity**: MEDIUM
- **Large items**: Especially daunting
- **Hidden costs**: High delivery costs add up
- **Deal breaker**: Unaffordable after fees
- **Source**: Home Furnishings Association

### 6. Product Quality Uncertainty
- **Severity**: HIGH
- **Pre-purchase**: Cannot physically inspect
- **Misrepresentation**: Fear of inaccurate descriptions
- **Photos**: May not reflect actual condition
- **Returns**: Complex process if dissatisfied

## COMMON FRAUD SCENARIOS (2024-2026 Data)

### 1. Payment App Scams
- **Severity**: CRITICAL
- **Platforms**: Zelle, Venmo, CashApp
- **Method**: Fake payment confirmations
- **Protection**: Minimal, transactions irreversible
- **Trend**: Increasing, doubled in 2022
- **Source**: AARP, Keeper Security

### 2. Overpayment Scams
- **Severity**: HIGH
- **Method**: Overpay + request refund of difference
- **Outcome**: Chargeback filed, seller loses both item and money
- **Target**: Inexperienced sellers
- **Source**: Unit21, Norton LifeLock

### 3. Verification Code Scams
- **Severity**: CRITICAL
- **Method**: Request phone number, send verification code
- **Claim**: "Verify identity before transaction"
- **Result**: Account takeover or identity theft
- **Prevention**: Never share codes, stay on platform
- **Source**: Keeper Security, CommunityAmerica

### 4. Triangulation Fraud
- **Severity**: HIGH
- **Method**: Fake store lists legitimate goods
- **Process**: Uses stolen cards to buy from real sellers
- **Ships**: To customer (appears legitimate)
- **Impact**: Legitimate sellers hit with chargebacks
- **Source**: Sift, Unit21

### 5. Fake Listings
- **Severity**: HIGH
- **Growth**: Doubled on Facebook Marketplace in 2022
- **Method**: Non-existent products or misrepresented items
- **Payment**: Request advance payment, never deliver
- **Source**: F-Secure, Avast research

### 6. Duplicate/Impersonation Accounts
- **Severity**: MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Method**: Easy account creation enables bad actors
- **Impact**: Reputation damage from repeat offenders
- **Prevention**: Stricter identity verification needed
- **Source**: Sift fraud prevention guide

### 7. First-Party Fraud
- **Severity**: CRITICAL
- **Growth**: 36% of all fraud in 2024 (up from 15% in 2023)
- **Method**: Legitimate users abusing returns, chargebacks
- **Detection**: Hardest to prevent (authorized users)
- **Source**: Sift Q1 2024 report

## COMMUNICATION PREFERENCES

### Mobile Communication
- **Preferred**: In-app messaging
- **Speed**: Quick responses expected (61% act within 1 hour)
- **Session length**: Average 3.5 minutes
- **Use cases**: Initial contact, quick questions, scheduling
- **Risk**: More vulnerable to scams if moved off-platform

### Desktop Communication
- **Preferred**: Detailed negotiations
- **Session depth**: More pages visited, longer time per visit
- **Use cases**: Complex transactions, document review
- **Advantage**: Enhanced security features
- **Source**: Semrush, Go-Globe research

### Platform Behavior
- **Never share phone numbers**: Communicate through app only
- **Verification**: Avoid sharing codes or personal info
- **Payment**: Keep transactions on platform when possible
- **Red flags**: Requests to move off-platform

## MOBILE VS DESKTOP USAGE PATTERNS

### Traffic Distribution (2026)
- **Mobile**: 63.15% of global web traffic
- **Desktop**: 36.85% of global web traffic
- **Retail sector**: 72% mobile traffic, but only 46% conversions
- **Source**: Research.com, Digital Silk

### Conversion Patterns
- **Desktop AOV**: $122 (higher value transactions)
- **Mobile AOV**: $86 (lower value, convenience)
- **Mobile abandonment**: 83% cart abandonment
- **Desktop abandonment**: 67% cart abandonment
- **Source**: SQ Magazine, Semrush

### User Behavior by Device

**Mobile Users:**
- Quick browsing and discovery
- Price research and comparisons
- Location-based searches
- 61% take action within 1 hour of search
- Shorter sessions (3.5 min average)
- Higher friction in purchasing

**Desktop Users:**
- Detailed product research
- Complex comparisons
- Higher-value purchases
- More pages per visit
- Longer session duration
- Better for transactions requiring security

### Cross-Device Journey
- **70% pattern**: Start on mobile, switch to desktop for completion
- **Reason**: Enhanced security, attention to detail, high-value transactions
- **Implication**: Need seamless cross-device experience
- **Source**: Storyly, Alexander Jarvis research

## KEY RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CLASSIFIEDS PLATFORMS

### 1. Trust & Safety (Priority: CRITICAL)
- Implement mandatory identity verification (KYC/KYB)
- Use document verification, biometric checks
- Monitor for duplicate/impersonation accounts
- Activity monitoring and link analysis
- Manual review for high-risk transactions

### 2. Fraud Prevention (Priority: CRITICAL)
- Multi-layered verification at onboarding
- Transaction monitoring and anomaly detection
- Maintain <1.3% payment fraud rate (Sift benchmark)
- <0.024% fraudulent chargeback rate (industry best)
- First-party fraud detection (36% of all fraud)

### 3. Payment Security (Priority: HIGH)
- Integrated payment systems with fraud protection
- Avoid directing users to P2P apps (Zelle, Venmo)
- Escrow services for high-value items
- Chargeback protection for sellers
- Credit card as preferred method (buyer protection)

### 4. Mobile Optimization (Priority: HIGH)
- Reduce cart abandonment from 83%
- Streamline checkout (fewer fields)
- Quick actions within 1 hour window
- Seamless cross-device experience
- Mobile-first messaging, desktop for transactions

### 5. Seller Support (Priority: MEDIUM-HIGH)
- AI-powered pricing assistants
- Listing optimization tools
- Differentiation features (badges, verification)
- Review encouragement systems
- Lower commission fees for verified sellers

### 6. Communication Safety (Priority: HIGH)
- Keep all communication on-platform
- Block phone number/email sharing
- Automated scam pattern detection
- Warning systems for risky behavior
- Report and block features
