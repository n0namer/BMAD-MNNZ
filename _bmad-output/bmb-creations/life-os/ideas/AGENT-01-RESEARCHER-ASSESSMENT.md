## IDEA-001: Katana-VectorBT (Automated Trading Platform)

### Market Assessment
- **TAM:** $50+ billion (global algo-trading + retail trading platforms)
- **SAM (Serviceable):** $2-5 billion (retail trading strategies + small institutions)
- **Growth Rate:** 18-22% annually
- **Market Maturity:** Growing

### Competitive Landscape
- **Direct Competitors:** QuantConnect, TradingView, zipline, Interactive Brokers API
- **Our Competitive Advantage:**
  1. Mass Optimization focus (52 parameters, 665k+ backtests)
  2. Python-first, optimized for speed
  3. Real-time to Scaled-Live pipeline (90-day vs 6-12 months industry)

### Target Customer
- **Profile:** Retail traders/strategy developers (Python-fluent, risk-averse)
- **Pain Points:** Manual optimization weeks, unclear viability framework, difficult scaling to live
- **Willingness to Pay:** $50-500/month (subscription) or 20-30% revenue share

### Revenue Model
- **Primary:** Subscription ($50-200/month per developer)
- **Secondary:** Revenue share on Scaled-Live strategies (20-30%)
- **Unit Economics:** LTV/CAC ~ 8:1, Margins 70-80%
- **Revenue Potential:** $200-500k/month at scale

### Scalability
- **Geographic Expansion:** High (digital product, global)
- **Vertical Expansion:** Crypto, multi-asset, white-label
- **Network Effects:** Moderate
- **Challenges:** Regulatory uncertainty, infrastructure costs, live trading risk

### Market Risks
- 🚩 **CRITICAL:** Regulatory (SEC may classify strategy recommendations as investment advice)
- ⚠️ **MEDIUM:** Competition intensifying
- ⚠️ **MEDIUM:** Market volatility impacts customer confidence
- ℹ️ **OPPORTUNITY:** AI-powered strategy generation

### Recommendation
- **Market Score:** 8.5/10
- **Confidence:** HIGH
- **Recommendation:** PROCEED (highest market potential in portfolio)
- **Next Steps:** Validate regulatory classification, beta with QuantConnect users, revenue-share partnerships
