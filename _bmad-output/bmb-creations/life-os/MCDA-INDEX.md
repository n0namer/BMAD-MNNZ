## 🎓 SCORING METHODOLOGY

**MCDA = Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis**

### 5 Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale each)

| Criterion | Definition | High Score (5) | Low Score (1) |
|-----------|-----------|-------|-------|
| **IMPACT** | Revenue/market potential | Exceptional market size, high WTP | Minimal market, limited growth |
| **CONFIDENCE** | Team capability | Proven skills, existing assets | Unproven, requires learning |
| **EFFORT** | Resource needs | Low effort, quick win | High effort, long timeline |
| **ALIGNMENT** | Strategic fit with 2026 goals | Perfect fit, synergistic | Misaligned, distracting |
| **RISK** | Risk mitigation quality | Well-mitigated, clear path | Unmitigated, uncertain |

**Note:** EFFORT and RISK criteria are INVERTED (low effort/risk = high score)

### Calculation
```
Score = (Impact + Confidence + Effort + Alignment + Risk) / 5 × 2.5
Scale: 0-10 (capped at 10)
```
