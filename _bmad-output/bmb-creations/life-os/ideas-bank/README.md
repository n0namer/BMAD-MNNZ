# Ideas Bank

The Ideas Bank is your centralized repository for capturing, evaluating, and managing ideas across all life spheres before they become projects.

## Purpose

- Capture ideas quickly without losing context
- Evaluate ideas systematically using prioritization criteria
- Plan implementation approach before committing resources
- Archive ideas with clear decision rationale

## Folder Structure

### inbox/
**Purpose**: Quick capture zone for raw ideas

- Drop ideas here immediately when they occur
- Minimal metadata required (just enough to remember the idea)
- Regular triage moves ideas to evaluated/ or archive/

### evaluated/
**Purpose**: Ideas that have been assessed and scored

- Contains ideas with completed evaluation scores
- Prioritization criteria applied (sphere alignment, impact, effort, timing)
- Decision pending on whether to plan or archive

### planned/
**Purpose**: Ideas with detailed implementation plans

- Contains ideas with complete action plans
- Ready to activate into projects when resources available
- Includes roles, timelines, success criteria

### archive/
**Purpose**: Ideas that won't become projects (with reasons)

Subfolders:
- **rejected/**: Ideas that don't align with goals or aren't worth pursuing
- **postponed/**: Good ideas but wrong timing (review quarterly)
- **activated/**: Ideas successfully converted to projects (reference only)

## Idea Lifecycle

```
1. CAPTURE  → inbox/           (quick entry, minimal metadata)
2. EVALUATE → evaluated/       (score and prioritize)
3. PLAN     → planned/         (detailed breakdown)
4. DECIDE   → archive/* or projects-bank/active/
```

## Template

Use `templates/idea.template.md` to create new ideas with proper frontmatter structure.

## Triage Schedule

- **Daily**: Review inbox/ (5 min)
- **Weekly**: Evaluate 3-5 ideas from inbox/
- **Monthly**: Plan 1-2 top-scored ideas
- **Quarterly**: Review postponed/ for reactivation

## Best Practices

1. Capture ideas immediately in inbox/ without overthinking
2. Batch evaluate ideas (more efficient than one-by-one)
3. Be ruthless in archiving (most ideas should NOT become projects)
4. Include "why not now" reasoning when postponing
5. Link activated ideas to their project folders for traceability
