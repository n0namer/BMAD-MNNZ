## 📋 MCDA METHODOLOGY

**Criteria (1-5 scale):**
1. **IMPACT:** Revenue/market potential, TAM, scalability
2. **CONFIDENCE:** Team capability, assets, feasibility
3. **EFFORT:** Resource needs [INVERTED: low effort = high score]
4. **ALIGNMENT:** Strategic fit with 2026 goals
5. **RISK:** Mitigation quality [INVERTED: well-mitigated = high score]

**Formula:** (I + C + E + A + R) / 5 × 2.5 = Score/10

**Confidence levels:**
- 95%+: Katana (data-driven, phases complete)
- 85%+: Sales QA, Recipes (market validated, clear path)
- 70%+: DepylBrazil (SMART goals set, commitment pending)
- 60%-: Consilium (consumer SaaS, PMF unproven)
- <60%: Maps Bot, VK Bot (unvalidated, high execution risk)
