## 🎯 CALL AGENDA (60 MINUTES)

### Part 1: Context Setting (5 min)

**Your Opening:**
> "Thanks for the call, Galina. I've completed my 72-hour analysis. I see strong potential here, and I want to discuss specific conditions that would make this work well for both of us. I have 4 key topics: brand clarity, payment structure, timeline, and role boundaries. Sound good?"

**Expected Response:**
- ✅ "Yes, let's discuss" → PROCEED
- ⚠️ "We're still figuring things out" → CAUTION (not ready for your involvement)
- ❌ "Can we talk about this next week?" → NO-GO (missing 72h deadline)

---

### Part 2: Brand Clarity (15 min)

**MUST-HAVE: Clear answer on DepylBrazil vs Saxap**

#### Question 1:
> "I noticed in the materials there's mention of both DepylBrazil and Saxap. Can you clarify: Are we franchising one brand or two? What's the relationship between them?"

**LISTEN FOR:**

✅ **GREEN FLAG (GO):**
- Clear answer: "DepylBrazil is the franchise brand, Saxap is our supplier" OR
- Clear answer: "We're franchising both, but as separate product lines" OR
- Clear answer: "We're consolidating under DepylBrazil, Saxap being phased out"

⚠️ **YELLOW FLAG (CAUTION):**
- Uncertain: "We're still deciding the brand strategy"
- Conflicting: "Some locations will be DepylBrazil, others Saxap"

❌ **RED FLAG (NO-GO):**
- No clear answer after 5 minutes of discussion
- Different answer than last conversation (inconsistency)
- "It doesn't really matter, they're both the same"

#### Question 2:
> "When would the brand strategy decision be finalized? I ask because I can't design the org structure or CRM without knowing which brand(s) we're working with."

**ACCEPTABLE ANSWERS:**
- ✅ "It's already decided, let me clarify now" (BEST)
- ✅ "We'll finalize it in week 1, before you start org structure work" (OK)
- ⚠️ "Probably by week 2" (RISKY - delays your critical path work)
- ❌ "We'll figure it out as we go" (NO-GO - can't work without foundation)

**CHECKLIST:**
- [ ] Brand strategy is clear (DepylBrazil only OR both OR segmentation)
- [ ] Decision timeline: Week 1 or already decided
- [ ] Galina can articulate the strategy in 2-3 sentences

**If 0/3 checked → STRONG NO-GO SIGNAL**

---

### Part 3: Payment Structure (20 min)

**MUST-HAVE: Locked agreement before leaving call**

#### Present Your Proposal:

**Option A: Fixed + KPI (Recommended)**
> "I propose a Fixed + KPI structure:
> - Base fee: $3,500 for org structure and CRM architecture
>   - 50% ($1,750) upfront when we sign the contract
>   - 50% ($1,750) on delivery in 35-40 days
> - KPI Bonus 1: $1,000 when CRM goes live (proves my design works)
> - KPI Bonus 2: $1,500 when first franchise sells (proves package works)
> - Total potential: $6,000 over 6 months
>
> This structure protects both of us: you get quality work with skin in the game, I get guaranteed base plus upside if the franchise succeeds. Thoughts?"

**Option B: Pilot → Partnership (If you prefer upside)**
> "Alternative structure I'd consider:
> - Pilot fee: $3,000 for 30-day engagement
> - Partnership option after pilot: 5% equity OR 2% of gross franchise revenue
> - Vesting over 2 years (50% year 1, 50% year 2)
>
> This aligns us long-term if we work well together. Which structure interests you more?"

#### LISTEN FOR:

✅ **GREEN FLAG (GO):**
- "I like the Fixed+KPI structure, let's lock that in"
- "The partnership option is interesting, let's discuss the details"
- Asks clarifying questions about payment timing (shows understanding)
- "Can we sign the contract today/tomorrow?" (urgency = commitment)

⚠️ **YELLOW FLAG (CAUTION):**
- "Can we start with lower base and higher KPIs?" (negotiate, but watch total)
- "Can payment be net-30 after delivery?" (delays cash flow, but might be OK)
- "We need to discuss with our accountant" (reasonable, but set deadline)

❌ **RED FLAG (NO-GO):**
- "We were thinking more like $1,000 total" (massively undervalues your work)
- "Can we pay you when franchise sales come in?" (all risk on you, no base)
- "Let's start and figure out payment later" (working for free risk)
- "We need another week to decide on budget" (misses 72h deadline)

#### Negotiation Boundaries:

**Minimum Acceptable:**
- Base fee: $2,500 minimum (below this, opportunity cost too high)
- Upfront: At least 30% upfront (de-risks your time)
- Total potential: $4,000+ (including bonuses/upside)

**Walk-Away Numbers:**
- If total < $3,000 → NO-GO (not worth off-brand work)
- If 0% upfront → NO-GO (all risk on you)
- If payment "TBD" after call → NO-GO (violates 72h deadline)

#### Question 3:
> "Assuming we agree on structure today, when can we have a signed contract? I'd like to lock this in before I invest more time."

**ACCEPTABLE ANSWERS:**
- ✅ "I can send contract draft tomorrow, sign by end of week" (GOOD)
- ✅ "Let me send you contract template today, you review and we finalize" (GOOD)
- ⚠️ "We'll need a few days to draft the contract" (OK, but set deadline)
- ❌ "We don't usually do contracts, can we handshake?" (NO - no protection)
- ❌ "Let's start work first, contract can come later" (NO - working without agreement)

**CHECKLIST:**
- [ ] Payment structure agreed (Fixed+KPI, Pilot→Partnership, or acceptable alternative)
- [ ] Total payment ≥ $4,000
- [ ] Upfront component ≥ 30% (if fixed fee model)
- [ ] Contract timeline: Draft within 2-3 days, signed within 7 days
- [ ] Payment schedule clear (when you receive each portion)

**If 0-2/5 checked → NO-GO**
**If 3/5 checked → CONDITIONAL (need improvements)**
**If 4-5/5 checked → GO (payment cleared)**

---

### Part 4: Timeline Realism (10 min)

**MUST-HAVE: 35-40 day timeline, not 30 days**

#### Your Position:
> "I've mapped out the critical path, and I want to set realistic expectations. With my involvement, I believe 35-40 days is achievable. 30 days is optimistic even with me, and could set us up for blame if things slip. Are you OK with a 35-40 day timeline?"

**LISTEN FOR:**

✅ **GREEN FLAG (GO):**
- "That makes sense, let's plan for 35-40 days with milestones"
- "I appreciate the honesty. 30 days was aggressive anyway"
- Asks about milestone structure (shows project management thinking)

⚠️ **YELLOW FLAG (CAUTION):**
- "Can we aim for 30 but have 40 as buffer?" (OK if framed as buffer, not deadline)
- "We have pressure to deliver in 30 days" (understand where pressure comes from)

❌ **RED FLAG (NO-GO):**
- "No, it HAS to be 30 days, non-negotiable" (unrealistic pressure)
- "If you can't do 30, maybe you're not the right person" (blame setup)
- "The team said 45 days without you, so 30 with you makes sense" (bad math)

#### Question 4:
> "What happens if we hit 30 days and we're 80% done but need another week? Is there flexibility, or is day 30 a hard stop?"

**ACCEPTABLE ANSWERS:**
- ✅ "We have flexibility, quality matters more than hitting arbitrary date" (GOOD)
- ✅ "Day 30 is target, but we can adjust if we're making good progress" (GOOD)
- ⚠️ "We'd need to discuss if we're past 30 days" (OK, but vague)
- ❌ "Day 30 is final, no exceptions" (Unrealistic and blame setup)

**CHECKLIST:**
- [ ] Timeline accepted as 35-40 days (not 30)
- [ ] Milestone structure acknowledged (week 1-2 foundation, 3-4 implementation, 5-6 completion)
- [ ] Flexibility if quality requires extra few days
- [ ] No blame risk if realistic delays occur

**If 0-1/4 checked → NO-GO (timeline pressure too high)**
**If 2-3/4 checked → GO (realistic expectations)**

---

### Part 5: Role Boundaries (10 min)

**MUST-HAVE: Clear scope (design, not implementation)**

#### Your Position:
> "Let me clarify my role to avoid scope creep. I'll deliver:
> 1. Functional org structure (roles, responsibilities, measurement)
> 2. CRM architecture (workflows, automations, role definitions)
> 3. Light coordination with the Bitrix specialist
>
> I will NOT be doing:
> - Bitrix24 hands-on implementation (that's the specialist)
> - Training content creation (that's Galina + specialist)
> - Day-to-day project management (I'm advisor, not PM)
> - Full-time availability (I'll be ~1 hour/day avg, flexible)
>
> Does this match your expectations?"

**LISTEN FOR:**

✅ **GREEN FLAG (GO):**
- "Yes, that's exactly what we need"
- "Who will do the implementation then?" (shows they understand division)
- "We have a Bitrix specialist lined up" (reduces your scope risk)

⚠️ **YELLOW FLAG (CAUTION):**
- "Can you also help with implementation a bit?" (small additions OK, watch total hours)
- "We might need you more involved in week 3-4" (clarify what "more involved" means)

❌ **RED FLAG (NO-GO):**
- "We thought you'd be doing the full Bitrix setup" (scope mismatch)
- "We need you full-time for 30 days" (not 16-25 hours, that's 100+ hours)
- "Can you project manage the whole thing?" (massive scope creep)

#### Question 5:
> "Do you have a Bitrix24 specialist identified who can do the hands-on implementation? And what's the budget for that 70-130 hour specialist role?"

**ACCEPTABLE ANSWERS:**
- ✅ "Yes, we have someone, budget confirmed at $X" (BEST)
- ✅ "We're interviewing specialists now, should have someone by week 2" (OK)
- ⚠️ "We thought you'd recommend someone" (reasonable, you can suggest, but not your scope)
- ❌ "We thought you'd do the implementation" (scope mismatch)
- ❌ "We don't have budget for specialist" (execution blocker)

**CHECKLIST:**
- [ ] Role scope clear: Design, not implementation
- [ ] Bitrix specialist identified or being recruited (not your job)
- [ ] Hours expectation: 16-25 hours over 30-40 days (~1 hr/day)
- [ ] Exit clauses if scope creep happens (you can stop if role expands without payment adjustment)

**If 0-2/4 checked → NO-GO (scope risk too high)**
**If 3-4/4 checked → GO (role clear)**

## 🎯 FINAL DECISION MATRIX

### At End of Call, Score Each Area:

| Area | Score | Status |
|------|-------|--------|
| **Brand Clarity** | ___/3 | ✅ 3/3 GO, ⚠️ 2/3 CAUTION, ❌ 0-1/3 NO-GO |
| **Payment Structure** | ___/5 | ✅ 4-5/5 GO, ⚠️ 3/5 CONDITIONAL, ❌ 0-2/5 NO-GO |
| **Timeline Realism** | ___/4 | ✅ 3-4/4 GO, ⚠️ 2/4 CAUTION, ❌ 0-1/4 NO-GO |
| **Role Boundaries** | ___/4 | ✅ 3-4/4 GO, ⚠️ 2/4 CAUTION, ❌ 0-1/4 NO-GO |
| **TOTAL** | ___/16 | |

### Decision Rules:

**GO Decision (13-16 points):**
- All 4 areas scored ✅ or ⚠️ (no ❌)
- Payment ≥ $4,000 total
- Contract timeline within 7 days
- **Action:** Say YES, move to contract drafting

**CONDITIONAL Decision (9-12 points):**
- 1-2 areas scored ❌, but fixable
- Example: Payment OK, but timeline pressure → Negotiate timeline
- Example: Brand unclear, but Galina commits to week 1 resolution → Set checkpoint
- **Action:** "I'm interested, but we need to address [specific issue] first. Can we resolve that by [date]?"

**NO-GO Decision (0-8 points):**
- 2+ areas scored ❌
- Payment < $3,000 OR payment timeline undefined
- Major red flags (brand confusion unfixable, scope mismatch, unrealistic timeline)
- **Action:** "Thanks for the opportunity, but this isn't the right fit right now. Happy to revisit if circumstances change."
